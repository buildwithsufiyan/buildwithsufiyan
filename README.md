### 💰 PaySathi — AI-Powered Personal Finance

PaySathi is a personal expense tracking and money-saving app concept designed to help users understand their spending, manage expenses, and make smarter financial decisions.

**Planned Features**
- 📊 Personal expense and income tracking
- 🤖 AI-powered savings suggestions
- 🧾 Receipt scanning and automatic expense categorization
- 💬 AI money assistant
- 🎙️ Hindi and Roman Urdu voice assistance
- 🔔 Unusual spending alerts

**Planned Platforms:** Android and Web  
**Technology Direction:** Python, Django, and AI integrations

**Status:** In development / prototype planning. PaySathi is not a UPI or payment-processing app, and it does not process real payments.
